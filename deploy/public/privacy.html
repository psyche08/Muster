What Muster does

Muster Agents, provided by the project publisher, connects your own devices so you can access authorized agent conversations, terminals and device services. Access depends on device pairing and permissions. This policy covers the Muster app and the default Muster relay service.

Data on your devices

The app stores device identity material, network membership, configuration, permissions, conversation caches, drafts and operation records as needed for its features. Device names, conversation content, terminal output and files you choose to access may be processed on your devices and transmitted to your authorized devices. Hardware-backed identity and presence keys use operating-system secure storage where supported. Biometric authentication is handled by the operating system; Muster does not receive your face or fingerprint data.

Connections and relay

Device-to-device service traffic is encrypted end to end. The relay forwards encrypted traffic and handles routing metadata, including device identifiers derived from public keys, connection addresses, online status and traffic timing. It also stores signed network membership records in a mailbox for synchronization; those records are not end-to-end encrypted and can include device public keys, names and permissions. For push wake-ups, the relay receives the device identifier and push token; Apple's push service delivers the notification. Service and diagnostic logs may contain device identifiers, connection metadata, errors and timing. These data support connectivity, reliability and troubleshooting; end-to-end encryption does not hide routing metadata.

Permissions and third-party services

The camera is used to scan pairing QR codes. Local-network access connects your devices. Notifications can wake the app. When you authorize access to a remote agent, terminal, browser or other service, that device and service receive the information needed to perform your request. AI providers and other services you use have their own privacy policies and retention rules. Installing Muster does not create an account with those services.

Tracking, support and TestFlight

Muster does not use advertising tracking or sell your personal information. If you contact support or send diagnostic material, we process the contact information and material you provide to investigate your request. Please remove secrets and unrelated personal information before sharing logs. TestFlight distribution and any feedback or crash reports shared through TestFlight are also subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Retention and your choices

Local records remain in the app's storage until removed by the applicable app controls or operating-system storage controls. Removing a device's authorization stops future authorized access; it does not erase copies already held by other devices or external services. Keychain items and backups may survive app removal under operating-system behavior. Relay routing and push data are maintained for service operation, and operational logs are retained for troubleshooting. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal data under our control. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised by this version of the service.

应用会在设备上保存功能所需的设备身份、网络成员信息、设置、权限、会话缓存、草稿及操作记录。你选择访问的会话、终端输出和文件可能在获授权设备之间传输。硬件支持的密钥使用系统安全存储；生物识别由操作系统处理，Muster 不接收人脸或指纹数据。

设备间服务流量采用端到端加密。Relay 转发密文，并处理公钥派生的设备标识、连接地址、在线状态、流量时间。用于同步的邮箱保存签名后的网络成员记录；这些记录并非端到端加密，可能包含设备公钥、名称及权限。推送唤醒会使用设备标识和推送令牌，并经 Apple 推送服务投递。服务和诊断日志可能包含设备标识、连接元数据、错误和时间信息，用于连接、稳定性及排障。端到端加密并不隐藏路由元数据。

相机用于扫描配对二维码，本地网络权限用于连接设备，通知用于唤醒。你主动使用的 AI、终端、浏览器等服务会收到执行请求所需的信息，并适用各自的隐私政策。Muster 不进行广告跟踪，不出售个人信息。主动提交的联系方式和诊断资料用于处理支持请求；发送日志前请移除密钥及无关个人信息。TestFlight 反馈与崩溃报告也适用 Apple 的隐私规则。

本地数据由应用或系统存储管理功能删除。撤销设备权限不会删除其他设备或外部服务已持有的副本；钥匙串项目和备份可能在卸载后保留。Relay 路由及推送数据用于维持服务，运行日志用于排障；当前版本未承诺固定自动删除期限。对于我们控制的个人数据，可通过 发布者配置的私密联系渠道 {{PRIVATE_SUPPORT_CONTACT}} 申请访问、更正或删除。政策变化时会更新本页及生效日期。
