Muster Agents

Support / 使用与支持

Muster connects your iPhone or iPad to your own authorized devices to view agent conversations and use remote device services.

Getting started

  1. Install Muster on an Apple-silicon Mac running macOS 14 or later from the download page. iPhone and iPad require iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.
  2. Open Muster on the Mac, create a network, and display a device invitation.
  3. On iPhone or iPad, choose “Join a network” and scan the invitation QR code. Allow camera and local-network access when needed.
  4. Approve the new device on the administrator's device and grant only the permissions you intend to use.
  5. Keep the host Mac online. Agent or terminal features require the corresponding application or service on that Mac and its own authorization.

Muster has no central username/password sign-in. Device pairing is required for remote features. You do not need to provide support with an invitation, private key, account password or full conversation history.

Troubleshooting

Check that both devices are online, the host is running Muster, and the requested device permission is enabled. For a failed or uncertain send, inspect the conversation on the host before sending again, to avoid duplicates.

Contact

For non-sensitive bugs, open a project issue with the app version/build, device and OS version, the time of the problem and a short description. Public issues are visible to others. Do not include personal data or credentials; use the publisher’s private contact channel for privacy requests.
